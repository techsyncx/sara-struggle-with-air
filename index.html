Challenge 1: Sara’s Struggle with Air

Sara is a 29-year-old schoolteacher living in Lahore, one of the most polluted cities in Pakistan. Every morning, she cycles a short distance to her school, but by the time she arrives, her throat feels scratchy and her chest heavy.

She has started experiencing frequent migraines and shortness of breath—symptoms her doctor says are aggravated by smog and poor air quality.

Her Challenges

  • She sees the Air Quality Index numbers (153, 220, 300) but doesn’t understand what they mean.
  • She doesn’t know when it’s safe for her students to play outside.
  • She’s unsure if masks or avoiding cycling make a real difference.

Sara feels trapped — forced to choose between her health and her daily routine.
